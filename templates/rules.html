Tournament Regulations

Player Eligibility

All participating players must be officially registered before the tournament starts. No unregistered player will be allowed to participate in any match.

Match Duration

Each match consists of two halves of 20 minutes each with a halftime break of 5 minutes. Tournament organizers may adjust timing if necessary.

Discipline

Violence, insults, misconduct or dangerous play can result in suspension or complete disqualification from the tournament in the worst situations police intervention will take palce.

Substitutions

Each team is allowed substitutions according to tournament regulations. Players substituted may return depending on match rules unless ruled out by the match rules.

Referee Authority

Referee decisions during matches are final. Any protest must be submitted officially through tournament management.

Qualification Rules

Teams qualify based on points, goal difference and goals scored during the group stage. Top teams proceed to knockout rounds.
