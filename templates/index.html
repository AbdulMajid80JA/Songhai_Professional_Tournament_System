About The Tournament

The Songhai Sallah Football Gala Tournament is a community-driven competition that brings together talented football teams, passionate fans and youth across different communities. The tournament promotes discipline, unity, sportsmanship and opportunity while creating an environment for players to showcase their abilities and cultural pride.
